# T-Slot Accessory Compatibility Article Design

## Goal

Publish a practical, multilingual buyer guide that helps industrial buyers verify whether T-slot profiles, fasteners, brackets, and related accessories will fit before ordering.

## Audience and positioning

The article serves buyers and integrators who have a profile family, partial drawing, or sample but need to check mating interfaces and purchasing scope. Lingye is presented as a sourcing coordinator, not as the manufacturer or owner of universal compatibility data.

## Content approach

Use the working title “T-Slot Aluminium Profile Compatibility: How to Check Slots, Fasteners and Accessories Before Ordering.” Explain that nominal profile labels such as 2020, 3030, and 4040 do not by themselves establish slot geometry or accessory compatibility. Organize the guide around five buyer actions: identify the exact profile and slot, verify fastener and bracket interfaces, assess mixed-series assumptions, test a representative assembly, and prepare a complete RFQ checklist. Include a worked example that distinguishes confirmed facts from open questions. Avoid unverified dimensions, brand equivalence claims, load ratings, and universal standards claims.

## Visuals and localization

Create two original technical illustrations: an interface map showing a slot/profile, T-nut/bolt, bracket, and end connector as separate mating interfaces; and a copy-ready RFQ checklist graphic. Publish full English, Simplified Chinese, Latin American Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese versions, preserving the same article structure and technical caveats. Localize titles, summaries, metadata, alt text, and internal links.

## Publishing and validation

Follow the existing article publishing command and translation dictionary patterns. Link to the live T-slot product/accessory category and relevant existing T-slot guides. Verify JSON and HTML integrity, language availability through the live API, images and links, localized sitemap URLs, and rendered article routes. Back up affected production files before deployment.

## Out of scope

- Claiming compatibility between named third-party brands or profile series without verified dimensions.
- Publishing dimensional tables, load ratings, or engineering recommendations not supported by product drawings or authoritative references.
- Replacing or modifying existing product specifications.
